# A Brief History of Potsdam

Potsdam has a 1,000-year history marked by Slavic roots, Prussian royal splendor, and 20th-century geopolitical turning points:

* **Early Origins (993):** First written about as *Poztupimi* in a document signed by Emperor Otto III.
* **Prussian Royal Residence (17th–18th Century):** Elector Frederick William and later Frederick the Great transformed it into a lavish Baroque and Rococo garrison and palace city (including Sanssouci Palace).
* **Imperial & Weimar Era (to 1918):** Served as a seat of Prussian kings and German emperors until the end of World War I, and saw the symbolic opening of the Reichstag by Hindenburg and Hitler in 1933.
* **World War II & The Potsdam Conference (1945):** Severely damaged by Allied bombs, the city hosted the Potsdam Conference at Cecilienhof Palace, where Allied leaders shaped post-war Germany.
* **Cold War & Modern Day (1990–Present):** Trapped in East Germany (GDR) near the Glienicke Bridge, it became the capital of the state of Brandenburg after German reunification, and its vast palaces and parks were named a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
