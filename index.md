# How Potsdam Is Governed

## 1. City Hall & Lord Mayor
Potsdam is an independent city (*kreisfreie Stadt*) led by **Lord Mayor Noosha Aubel**. She oversees municipal administration, public services, and county-level duties from Potsdam City Hall.

## 2. City Council & Neighborhoods
The **City Council** (*Stadtverordnetenversammlung*) includes 56 elected councillors across twelve political groups. Nine local district councils (*Ortsteile*) also represent neighborhood interests.
