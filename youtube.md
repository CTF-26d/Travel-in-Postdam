### 🏰 Sanssouci Palace & Park
Frederick the Great’s Rococo summer palace and its terraced gardens are Potsdam’s most famous UNESCO-listed attraction. A good video introduction to the palace, interiors, and park setting.
**YouTube:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lyR36fdooSo>

### 🕊️ Cecilienhof Palace
This English Tudor-style palace is historically important as the site of the 1945 Potsdam Conference. The official Prussian Palaces and Gardens Foundation channel has videos about the palace and its history.
**YouTube:** <https://www.youtube.com/c/spsgmuseum>

### 🧱 Dutch Quarter (Holländisches Viertel)
A picturesque neighborhood of red-brick Dutch-style houses built in the 18th century. It’s great for strolling, cafés, and photography; this YouTube search link leads to walking-tour videos.
**YouTube:** <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Potsdam+Dutch+Quarter+walking+tour>

### 🏛️ Brandenburg Gate (Potsdam)
Potsdam’s own neoclassical city gate, smaller and less crowded than Berlin’s famous gate, sits at the end of the main shopping street. This search link shows short videos and city-walk clips featuring it.
**YouTube:** <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Potsdam+Brandenburg+Gate>

### 👑 New Palace (Neues Palais)
The large Baroque palace at the far end of Sanssouci Park, built under Frederick the Great. It is impressive both architecturally and for its richly decorated interiors.
**YouTube:** <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Potsdam+New+Palace+Neues+Palais>
